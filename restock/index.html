Restock

pantry & receipts

Restock learns how often you buy each item from your purchase dates and receipts. After two purchases it predicts the next one. Set a fixed interval on any item to override. The slider on each row is your say: Restocked restarts the cycle from today, Buy soon puts it on the list.
